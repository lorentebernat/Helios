# 26 — Specialist Encyclopedia

> This chapter is a strategic reference for the specialist system described throughout the source guide. Rather than listing every individual specialist in the game, it explains the major specialist categories, where they are used, and the types of bonuses they provide.

> **Note:** The source guide catalogs many individual specialists. This chapter summarizes those categories and their strategic uses rather than reproducing the entire item database.

---

# Overview

Specialists are unique items that dramatically improve different aspects of your empire.

They are assigned to:

- Trade Unions
- Town Halls
- Harbormaster's Offices
- Arctic Lodges
- Ships
- Expeditions

Late-game optimization depends heavily on effective specialist use. :contentReference[oaicite:0]{index=0}

---

# Specialist Categories

Most specialists improve one of the following areas:

- Production
- Population
- Logistics
- Military
- Harbor operations
- Expeditions
- Agriculture
- Research

Many provide multiple bonuses simultaneously.

---

# Trade Union Specialists

Purpose:

Improve industrial production.

Typical bonuses include:

- Increased productivity
- Additional goods
- Reduced workforce
- Reduced maintenance
- Alternative recipes
- Increased storage

Trade Union specialists often provide the largest production improvements available in the game.

---

# Productivity Specialists

Primary effect:

```text
Factory

↓

Higher Productivity

↓

More Goods

↓

Same Workforce
```

Benefits:

- Fewer factories required
- Lower maintenance per unit
- Greater industrial density

These are among the strongest general-purpose specialists.

---

# Additional Goods Specialists

Primary effect:

Produce bonus items alongside the normal output.

Example:

```text
Factory

↓

Normal Product

+

Bonus Product
```

Advantages:

- "Free" production
- Lower raw material cost per unit
- Higher profit

The guide consistently treats these as some of the most valuable production bonuses. :contentReference[oaicite:1]{index=1}

---

# Alternative Recipe Specialists

These specialists modify production recipes.

General concept:

```text
Original Recipe

↓

Alternative Recipe

↓

Same Output
```

Benefits:

- Simpler logistics
- Reduced imports
- Lower production costs

Alternative recipes become increasingly valuable as production chains grow.

---

# Workforce Specialists

Purpose:

Reduce workforce requirements.

Advantages:

- Easier expansion
- Less population pressure
- Higher efficiency

Especially useful for labor-intensive production chains.

---

# Maintenance Specialists

Purpose:

Reduce operating costs.

Benefits:

- Increased profitability
- Lower industrial expenses
- Easier scaling

Maintenance reductions are particularly valuable for large factory clusters.

---

# Agricultural Specialists

Applied through Trade Unions.

Typical effects:

- Higher farm productivity
- Reduced field requirements
- Additional harvests
- Improved efficiency

Large farming islands benefit greatly from agricultural specialists.

---

# Town Hall Specialists

Purpose:

Improve residential districts.

Possible bonuses include:

- Increased population
- Reduced consumption
- Increased happiness
- Higher tax income
- Improved attractiveness

Dense Engineer and Investor districts receive the greatest benefit.

---

# Population Specialists

Effects may include:

```text
Residence

↓

More Residents

↓

Higher Income
```

These specialists increase city density without constructing additional residences.

---

# Consumption Specialists

Purpose:

Reduce demand for goods.

Advantages:

- Lower production requirements
- Simpler logistics
- Greater stability

Reducing consumption often produces larger empire-wide savings than increasing production.

---

# Happiness Specialists

Possible effects:

- Increased happiness
- Reduced unrest
- Improved quality of life

Useful for maintaining stable high-tier populations.

---

# Harbormaster Specialists

Purpose:

Improve harbor operations.

Possible bonuses:

- Faster loading
- Additional goods
- Reduced maintenance
- Better repairs
- Improved harbor defenses

Major trade hubs benefit the most from these specialists.

---

# Naval Specialists

Some harbor specialists also improve:

- Military ships
- Harbor defenses
- Repair speed

These become more important in contested regions.

---

# Arctic Lodge Specialists

Exclusive to **The Passage** DLC.

Purpose:

Optimize Arctic production and logistics.

Functions similarly to Trade Union and Town Hall specialists but only within Arctic settlements. :contentReference[oaicite:2]{index=2}

---

# Ship Specialists

Ships may equip specialist items that provide:

- Higher speed
- Greater cargo capacity
- Improved combat
- Expedition bonuses
- Lower maintenance

Different ships benefit from different combinations.

---

# Expedition Specialists

Designed specifically for exploration.

Typical attributes:

- Navigation
- Medicine
- Hunting
- Diplomacy
- Crafting
- Faith

Balanced expedition teams significantly improve success rates.

---

# Cultural Specialists

Some specialists interact with:

- Museums
- Zoos
- Botanical Gardens

These improve:

- Attractiveness
- Tourism
- Collection bonuses

---

# Research Institute

The **Research Institute** allows players to:

- Duplicate specialists
- Research rare items
- Eliminate randomness from optimization

Duplicating the most valuable specialists is one of the strongest late-game strategies. :contentReference[oaicite:3]{index=3}

---

# Specialist Priorities

General priority order:

1. Additional goods
2. Alternative recipes
3. Productivity
4. Consumption reduction
5. Workforce reduction
6. Maintenance reduction

Actual priorities depend on the structure of your empire.

---

# Specialist Synergy

The strongest industrial layouts combine multiple effects.

Example:

```text
Trade Union

↓

Productivity

↓

Additional Goods

↓

Alternative Recipe

↓

Electricity

↓

Highly Optimized Production
```

Layering compatible specialists creates much greater gains than relying on a single bonus.

---

# Common Mistakes

⚠️ Ignoring specialists until the late game.

⚠️ Mixing unrelated factories under one Trade Union.

⚠️ Wasting Town Hall bonuses on sparse neighborhoods.

⚠️ Using low-impact specialists when stronger alternatives are available.

⚠️ Forgetting to duplicate valuable specialists with the Research Institute.

⚠️ Building new factories instead of optimizing existing ones.

# Key Takeaways

- Specialists are one of the largest sources of efficiency in Anno 1800.
- Additional goods and alternative recipes generally provide the greatest long-term value.
- Trade Union, Town Hall, and Harbormaster specialists each optimize different parts of your empire.
- The Research Institute transforms rare specialists into reproducible strategic assets.
- Expert play revolves around carefully combining complementary specialist effects rather than relying on raw production alone.
