# 11 — Buildings

> Buildings are the fundamental components of every settlement in **Anno 1800**. Every economic, logistical, military, and cultural system is built around specialized structures that fulfill distinct roles.

This chapter categorizes the major building types and explains their purpose within your empire.

---

# Building Categories

Buildings can be divided into the following groups:

- Residential
- Production
- Public Services
- Logistics
- Harbor
- Industry
- Cultural
- Government
- Military
- Monumental

As your population advances, additional buildings become available with each new tier. :contentReference[oaicite:0]{index=0}

---

# Residential Buildings

Residential buildings house your population.

Their functions are to:

- generate tax income
- provide workforce (except Investors)
- unlock progression
- determine service demand

Old World residences:

```text
Farmer Residence

↓

Worker Residence

↓

Artisan Residence

↓

Engineer Residence

↓

Investor Residence
```

Each upgrade increases both profitability and complexity.

# Marketplace

The Marketplace is the first essential public building.

Functions:

- satisfies basic needs
- supports population growth
- defines early residential districts

Nearly every settlement begins with one.

---

# Fire Station

Fire Stations protect nearby buildings.

Without coverage:

- fires spread
- production stops
- rebuilding costs increase

Industrial districts especially benefit from strong fire protection.

---

# Police Station

Police Stations reduce unrest.

Benefits include:

- public order
- safer working conditions
- fewer riots

Coverage becomes increasingly important as cities expand.

---

# Hospital

Hospitals combat disease outbreaks.

Large populations should maintain adequate medical coverage to reduce disruption.

---

# University

Universities become available with Artisans.

They unlock advanced education and later-game mechanics.

---

# Bank

Banks are introduced with Engineers.

Benefits include:

- increased income
- support for high-tier residences

Banks become especially valuable in dense Engineer and Investor districts.

---

# Warehouse

Warehouses are among the most important logistical buildings.

Functions:

- receive goods
- distribute goods
- store inventory

Poor warehouse placement is one of the most common causes of production bottlenecks.

---

# Harbor Buildings

Every island begins with a Trading Post.

Later harbor infrastructure includes:

- Depots
- Piers
- Repair Crane
- Harbor Defenses
- Harbormaster's Office
- Shipyards

Harbors become increasingly complex as global trade expands. :contentReference[oaicite:3]{index=3}

---

# Trade Union

Trade Unions improve nearby production buildings.

Specialist items may provide bonuses such as:

- increased productivity
- reduced workforce
- reduced maintenance
- alternative recipes
- additional goods

Factories should be clustered to maximize Trade Union coverage. :contentReference[oaicite:4]{index=4}

---

# Town Hall

Town Halls improve residential districts.

Possible specialist bonuses include:

- increased happiness
- reduced consumption
- increased income
- population bonuses

Dense residential neighborhoods benefit the most.

---

# Harbormaster's Office

Harbormaster's Offices affect nearby coastal buildings.

Potential bonuses include:

- improved harbor productivity
- faster loading
- reduced maintenance
- stronger naval defenses

---

# Arctic Lodge

The Arctic equivalent of:

- Trade Union
- Town Hall

It allows specialist items to enhance nearby Arctic buildings. :contentReference[oaicite:5]{index=5}

# Power Plant

Power Plants distribute electricity.

Typical network:

```text
Oil Harbor

↓

Railway

↓

Power Plant

↓

Industrial District
```

Industrial layouts should be designed around electricity coverage.

# Building Placement Principles

Good placement prioritizes:

- short transportation distances
- warehouse access
- electricity coverage
- specialist coverage
- future expansion

Poor placement often costs more than constructing additional buildings.

---

# Common Mistakes

⚠️ Building production too far from warehouses.

⚠️ Mixing heavy industry with dense residential districts.

⚠️ Ignoring Trade Union coverage.

⚠️ Blocking future railway routes.

⚠️ Building monuments before securing the economy.

⚠️ Underestimating warehouse capacity.

# Key Takeaways

- Every building should serve a clear strategic purpose.
- Organize buildings into specialized districts rather than mixing unrelated functions.
- Public services enable population growth, while production and logistics sustain it.
- Trade Unions, Town Halls, Harbormaster's Offices, and Arctic Lodges become increasingly valuable as your empire grows.
- Efficient building placement is one of the largest factors separating a functional city from a highly optimized one.
