# 27 — DLC Reference Guide

> This chapter summarizes the downloadable content (DLC) covered in the source guide and explains the strategic purpose of each expansion. Rather than listing every new building or production chain, it focuses on how each DLC changes the way your empire is developed.

> **Note:** This chapter only covers the DLCs described in the source guide.

---

# DLC Overview

The DLCs expand the game in four major ways:

- New regions
- New population tiers
- New production systems
- New optimization mechanics

Unlike the base game, most DLCs are designed to complement—not replace—the Old World economy.

---

# Sunken Treasures

## Main Feature

Cape Trelawney

---

## Purpose

Provides:

- The largest buildable island
- Additional Old World session
- Monument construction
- Mega-city development

The enormous central island makes it ideal for a capital city.

The guide recommends Cape Trelawney as the primary location for late-game expansion and cultural development. :contentReference[oaicite:0]{index=0}

Excellent for:

- Investors
- Cultural districts

- Monument projects

Cape Trelawney

Empire Capital

# Key Takeaways

- Every DLC adds new strategic layers rather than replacing existing mechanics.
- **Land of Lions** introduces the Research Institute and Scholars, making it one of the most influential expansions in the guide.
- **Sunken Treasures** provides an ideal location for a capital city, while **The Passage** introduces gas and air logistics.
- Later DLCs focus on optimization, tourism, density, and agricultural efficiency.
- The strongest empires integrate every DLC region into a single interconnected production and logistics network.
