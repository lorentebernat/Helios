# 29 — Ships & Naval Encyclopedia

> This chapter summarizes the naval mechanics presented throughout the source guide. Ships are the backbone of trade, exploration, colonization, and warfare. While individual statistics vary by vessel, the guide emphasizes choosing the right ship for the right role rather than building a single "best" fleet.

> **Note:** This chapter is a strategic overview based on the source guide. It does not attempt to reproduce every ship statistic or hidden game value.

---

# Overview

Ships perform four primary functions:

- Exploration
- Trade
- Military operations
- Expeditions

As your empire expands, naval logistics become one of the most important aspects of gameplay.

---

# Ship Categories

The guide divides ships into several broad categories:

- Exploration ships
- Cargo ships
- Military ships
- Steam ships
- Airships (The Passage / Empire of the Skies)

Each category fulfills a specialized role.

---

# Exploration Ships

Purpose:

- Discover islands
- Explore new regions
- Complete expeditions

Characteristics:

- Moderate speed
- Flexible equipment
- Good expedition capability

Exploration ships are usually your first vessels to leave the starting region.

---

# Cargo Ships

Primary purpose:

```text
Goods

↓

Trade Route

↓

Warehouse

↓

Consumers
```

Responsible for transporting:

- Raw materials
- Intermediate products
- Finished goods
- Construction materials

A stable empire depends on reliable cargo fleets.

---

# Military Ships

Purpose:

- Protect trade routes
- Defend harbors
- Escort expeditions
- Destroy enemy fleets

Military fleets should support the economy by ensuring uninterrupted logistics.

---

# Steam Ships

Steam-powered vessels gradually replace sailing ships.

Advantages include:

- Greater reliability
- Improved cargo capacity
- Better combat performance
- Faster travel

The guide consistently favors steam ships for demanding logistics and difficult expeditions.

---

# Airships

Available through Arctic progression and later DLC content.

Roles include:

- Mail
- Cargo
- Passenger transport

Airships supplement rather than replace naval logistics. :contentReference[oaicite:0]{index=0}

---

# Trade Routes

Typical logistics flow:

```text
Production Island

↓

Cargo Ship

↓

Harbor

↓

Consumer Island
```

Trade routes should be:

- Continuous
- Efficient
- Fully loaded

Half-empty ships waste maintenance and travel time.

---

# Fleet Composition

A mature empire typically operates:

- Exploration vessels
- Dedicated cargo ships
- Escort warships
- Expedition ship
- Specialized regional fleets

Different tasks require different ships.

---

# Expedition Ships

Ideal expedition vessels provide:

- High durability
- Good cargo capacity
- Multiple item slots

Before departure:

- Equip expedition specialists.
- Install expedition items.
- Repair the ship.

Preparation is more important than luck.

---

# Ship Items

Ships may equip items that improve:

- Speed
- Cargo capacity
- Combat ability
- Expedition success
- Repair rate

The guide frequently references ship items as part of successful expedition preparation. :contentReference[oaicite:1]{index=1}

---

# Harbor Infrastructure

Ships rely on efficient harbors.

Important buildings include:

- Trading Post
- Depot
- Pier
- Repair Crane
- Harbormaster's Office

Poor harbor design eventually limits the entire economy.

---

# Cargo Management

Good practice:

```text
Full Ship

↓

Single Destination

↓

Unload

↓

Reload

↓

Return
```

Avoid unnecessary intermediate stops whenever possible.

---

# Harbor Congestion

Symptoms include:

- Ships waiting offshore
- Slow unloading
- Production shortages
- Storage overflow

Solutions:

- Additional piers
- Better scheduling
- More specialized routes

---

# Route Specialization

Large empires benefit from dedicated routes.

Examples:

Old World ↔ New World

- Coffee
- Cotton
- Rubber

Old World ↔ Arctic

- Gas
- Supplies

Old World ↔ Enbesa

- Regional goods
- Research support

Each route should transport a limited set of goods whenever practical.

---

# Fleet Protection

Protect:

- Oil shipments
- Coffee imports
- Luxury goods
- Construction materials

Interruptions to major trade routes can quickly destabilize the economy.

---

# Naval Logistics Philosophy

The guide consistently emphasizes:

```text
Efficient Trade

↓

Stable Production

↓

Stable Population

↓

Economic Growth
```

Ships exist primarily to support production rather than warfare.

---

# Late-Game Fleet

Typical composition:

```text
Cargo Fleet

+

Escort Fleet

+

Expedition Vessel

+

Airships
```

Every vessel has a clearly defined purpose.

---

# Common Mistakes

⚠️ Using one ship type for every task.

⚠️ Overloading individual trade routes.

⚠️ Ignoring harbor congestion.

⚠️ Sending expeditions without specialists.

⚠️ Building more factories instead of improving shipping.

⚠️ Leaving trade routes unprotected.

# Key Takeaways

- Ships are the foundation of a global empire.
- Cargo fleets are more important than warships for long-term success.
- Steam ships become the preferred solution for demanding logistics and expeditions.
- Efficient trade routes and uncongested harbors are essential to maintaining stable production.
- Every ship should have a clearly defined role within the empire's logistics network.
