# 18 — Expeditions

> Expeditions are one of the primary exploration mechanics in **Anno 1800**. They allow you to discover new regions, unlock DLC sessions, obtain rare specialists, collect cultural items, and acquire unique rewards that cannot be produced through normal gameplay.

This chapter summarizes the expedition mechanics described in the source guide.

---

# Overview

Expeditions serve several important purposes:

- Discover new regions
- Unlock DLC content
- Obtain rare specialists
- Acquire cultural collection items
- Earn unique rewards

Many of the game's most valuable items are obtained through successful expeditions.

The guide references expeditions as the gateway to regions such as Cape Trelawney, the Arctic, and Enbesa, and also lists expedition-oriented ship items and specialists. :contentReference[oaicite:0]{index=0}

---

# Expedition Progression

Typical flow:

```text
Prepare Ship

↓

Assign Specialists

↓

Launch Expedition

↓

Resolve Events

↓

Receive Rewards
```

Preparation is usually more important than luck.

---

# Expedition Difficulty

Expeditions vary in difficulty.

Typical ratings:

- Easy
- Medium
- Hard

Higher difficulty generally offers better rewards but requires stronger preparation.

---

# Ship Selection

A successful expedition begins with choosing the right vessel.

Desirable characteristics include:

- High durability
- Adequate cargo capacity
- Appropriate item slots

As the game progresses, steam ships generally outperform early sailing vessels for demanding expeditions.

---

# Expedition Specialists

Specialists contribute abilities that help resolve expedition events.

Common attributes include:

- Navigation
- Diplomacy
- Hunting
- Medicine
- Crafting
- Faith

The guide includes numerous expedition-capable specialists with these types of bonuses. :contentReference[oaicite:1]{index=1}

---

# Ship Items

Ships can also equip expedition-focused items.

Typical bonuses include:

- Increased expedition success
- Better navigation
- Improved survivability
- Additional event options

Combining specialists and ship equipment creates the strongest expedition teams.

---

# Expedition Events

During an expedition, random events occur.

Examples include:

- Storms
- Illness
- Wildlife encounters
- Diplomatic meetings
- Navigation challenges

Each event may present multiple possible responses.

The success of each option depends largely on the abilities provided by your assigned specialists.

---

# Event Resolution

General process:

```text
Event

↓

Choose Response

↓

Skill Check

↓

Success or Failure
```

Selecting the option that matches your expedition's strengths usually provides the best outcome.

---

# Rewards

Possible expedition rewards include:

- Specialists
- Ship items
- Museum artifacts
- Zoo animals
- Botanical specimens
- Rare resources

Many cultural collections depend heavily on expedition rewards.

---

# Unlocking New Regions

Several DLC regions are unlocked through major expeditions.

Examples include:

## Cape Trelawney

Unlocked through the **Sunken Treasures** storyline after reaching the required Old World progression. :contentReference[oaicite:2]{index=2}

---

## Arctic

Unlocked through **The Passage** expedition. :contentReference[oaicite:3]{index=3}

---

## Enbesa

Unlocked through the **Land of Lions** expedition. :contentReference[oaicite:4]{index=4}

---

# Cultural Progression

Expeditions are one of the primary methods for completing:

- Museum collections
- Zoo collections
- Botanical collections

These collections later provide powerful empire-wide bonuses.

---

# Risk Management

Preparation reduces expedition risk.

Before departure, verify:

- Appropriate ship
- Strong specialists
- Useful ship items

Attempting difficult expeditions without adequate preparation often results in failure.

---

# Specialist Synergy

A balanced expedition team should cover multiple skills.

Example:

```text
Navigator

+

Doctor

+

Diplomat

+

Hunter
```

This broad coverage increases the chance of successfully resolving diverse events.

---

# Research Institute

Later in the game, the Research Institute can help reduce dependence on repeated expeditions by allowing certain specialists and items to be researched or duplicated.

This significantly changes late-game item acquisition. :contentReference[oaicite:5]{index=5}

---

# Optimization

Experienced players often:

- Reserve their best ship for expeditions.
- Keep expedition specialists available rather than assigning them permanently elsewhere.
- Equip expedition-focused ship items before departure.
- Prioritize high-value expeditions that unlock new regions or rare collections.

---

# Common Mistakes

⚠️ Launching expeditions without specialists.

⚠️ Ignoring expedition item slots.

⚠️ Using unsuitable ships.

⚠️ Choosing responses that do not match available skills.

⚠️ Selling rare expedition rewards needed for collections.

⚠️ Delaying major storyline expeditions that unlock new regions.

---

# Expedition Checklist

## Before Departure

- [ ] Appropriate ship selected.
- [ ] Specialists assigned.
- [ ] Ship items equipped.
- [ ] Cargo prepared (if required).

---

## During Expedition

- [ ] Match responses to available skills.
- [ ] Avoid unnecessary risks.
- [ ] Preserve ship health whenever possible.

---

## After Return

- [ ] Assign new specialists.
- [ ] Add collection items to cultural buildings.
- [ ] Review rewards before selling duplicates.

---

# Key Takeaways

- Expeditions unlock new regions, powerful specialists, and valuable cultural collections.
- Success depends primarily on preparation rather than chance.
- Balanced specialist teams dramatically improve event outcomes.
- Many of the game's strongest long-term bonuses originate from expedition rewards.
- Story expeditions are critical milestones for accessing Cape Trelawney, the Arctic, and Enbesa.
