# 10 — Population

> Population is the foundation of every empire in **Anno 1800**. Every production chain, public service, workforce requirement, and source of tax income is ultimately tied to your residents.

This chapter explains how population progresses, how needs work, and how to develop stable, profitable cities.

---

# Overview

Population serves four primary purposes:

- Generates income
- Provides workforce
- Unlocks buildings
- Unlocks new mechanics

Progression is driven by satisfying the needs of each social class.

---

# Old World Population

The primary population progression is:

```text
Farmers

↓

Workers

↓

Artisans

↓

Engineers

↓

Investors
```

Each promotion unlocks:

- new production chains
- additional public services
- more complex needs
- higher tax income
- advanced buildings

This tiered progression forms the backbone of the game's economic development. :contentReference[oaicite:0]{index=0}

# Workforce

Every population tier provides workforce for specific industries.

| Population | Typical Workforce |
|------------|-------------------|
| Farmers | Agriculture |
| Workers | Basic Industry |
| Artisans | Manufacturing |
| Engineers | Advanced Industry |
| Investors | None |

Investors no longer contribute workforce but generate significantly more income and Influence. :contentReference[oaicite:2]{index=2}

---

# Population Growth

Population increases by:

1. Constructing residences.
2. Supplying required goods.
3. Providing public services.
4. Upgrading residences.

Expansion should always follow production capacity.

---

# Needs

Every residence has two categories of needs.

## Basic Needs

Basic needs are required for:

- population growth
- residence upgrades
- progression

Examples vary by population tier.

---

## Luxury Needs

Luxury needs provide:

- additional tax income
- increased happiness
- greater stability

Luxury goods become increasingly important for Engineers and Investors. :contentReference[oaicite:3]{index=3}

---

# Residence Upgrades

Residences may be upgraded only after meeting the required conditions.

Typical process:

```text
Supply Needs

↓

Population Increases

↓

Upgrade Available

↓

Higher Population Tier

↓

New Needs
```

Each upgrade introduces additional complexity into your economy.

---

# Farmers

Farmers represent the foundation of every settlement.

Characteristics:

- agricultural workforce
- low tax income
- simple production
- minimal services

Typical responsibilities:

- food production
- timber
- fishing
- basic agriculture

---

# Workers

Workers unlock industrialization.

New requirements include:

- bricks
- steel
- bread
- soap
- schools
- police

They form the backbone of heavy industry during the early and mid game. :contentReference[oaicite:4]{index=4}

---

# Artisans

Artisans introduce significantly more advanced production.

Typical new demands include:

- canned food
- sewing machines
- fur coats
- rum
- university access

This is usually the point where inter-island trade becomes essential.

---

# Engineers

Engineers mark the transition to the industrial age.

Major unlocks include:

- electricity
- railways
- banks
- steam industry
- advanced manufacturing

Engineers consume many imported luxury goods and enable much of the late-game economy. :contentReference[oaicite:5]{index=5}

---

# Investors

Investors represent the highest Old World social class.

Characteristics:

- highest income
- no workforce
- increased Influence
- numerous luxury needs
- monument construction

The guide associates Investors with advanced cultural buildings and the World's Fair. :contentReference[oaicite:6]{index=6}

---

# Public Services

As populations advance, additional public buildings become mandatory.

Typical progression:

Farmers

- Marketplace
- Fire Station

Workers

- School
- Police

Artisans

- University

Engineers

- Bank

Investors

- World's Fair
- Cultural buildings

---

# Happiness

Meeting luxury needs improves:

- happiness
- tax income
- overall stability

Poorly supplied populations may experience reduced efficiency and slower development.

---

# Workforce Balance

Maintain:

```text
Available Workforce

>

Required Workforce
```

A workforce reserve provides flexibility for:

- expansion
- emergencies
- temporary shortages

Avoid operating continuously at zero available workers.

---

# Housing Density

Higher-tier residences generally generate:

- more income
- more population
- greater demand
- increased service requirements

Large investor districts become the financial engine of mature empires.

---

---

# Common Mistakes

⚠️ Upgrading residences too early.

⚠️ Ignoring luxury goods.

⚠️ Running negative workforce.

⚠️ Expanding population faster than production.

⚠️ Neglecting public services.

⚠️ Building housing without planning future upgrades.

# Key Takeaways

- Population drives every major system in Anno 1800.
- Meet basic needs before upgrading residences.
- Luxury goods dramatically improve income and stability.
- Workforce should always exceed demand.
- Higher population tiers generate greater wealth but require increasingly complex production chains and global logistics.
