# 17 — Museums, Zoos & Botanical Gardens

> Cultural buildings are one of the most powerful long-term progression systems in **Anno 1800**. While they initially appear to be decorative attractions, they eventually provide significant bonuses to attractiveness, tourism, research, production, and population through carefully assembled collections.

This chapter summarizes the cultural systems presented in the source guide.

---

# Overview

There are three primary cultural buildings:

- Museum
- Zoo
- Botanical Garden

Each building displays collectible sets that grant empire-wide bonuses when completed.

The guide includes extensive collection lists and explains the bonuses awarded for completing them. :contentReference[oaicite:0]{index=0}

---

# Purpose

Cultural buildings improve:

- City Attractiveness
- Tourism
- Population bonuses
- Productivity
- Research
- Prestige

Unlike production buildings, their value increases steadily throughout the late game.

---

# Cultural Progression

Typical development:

```text
Collect Items

↓

Complete Collections

↓

Increase Attractiveness

↓

Unlock Bonuses

↓

Improve Empire
```

Collections become increasingly valuable as your empire grows.

---

# Museum

Museums display historical and archaeological artifacts.

Typical themes include:

- Ancient civilizations
- Fossils
- Archaeology
- Historical relics

Completing museum collections grants permanent bonuses to your empire.

---

# Zoo

Zoos display animal collections gathered from expeditions and other sources.

Typical exhibits include:

- Mammals
- Birds
- Marine animals
- Predators
- Exotic wildlife

Complete animal sets provide additional attractiveness and unique gameplay bonuses.

---

# Botanical Garden

Botanical Gardens display plant collections.

Examples include:

- Tropical plants
- Temperate plants
- Rare flowers
- Exotic trees

Botanical collections improve attractiveness and often support tourism.

---

# Collections

Collections are groups of related items.

General workflow:

```text
Acquire Items

↓

Complete Set

↓

Receive Collection Bonus
```

Individual items have value, but completing entire collections provides the greatest rewards.

---

# Collection Bonuses

Depending on the collection, bonuses may include:

- Increased Attractiveness
- Production bonuses
- Population bonuses
- Research bonuses
- Tourism improvements

The guide highlights collection completion as the primary objective rather than simply displaying isolated items. :contentReference[oaicite:1]{index=1}

---

# Attractiveness

City Attractiveness is influenced by:

Positive factors:

- Cultural buildings
- Monuments
- Parks
- Landscaping

Negative factors:

- Heavy industry
- Pollution
- Certain industrial buildings

Maintaining high attractiveness is essential for advanced cultural gameplay.

---

# Tourism

High-attractiveness cities attract tourists.

Tourism benefits include:

- Additional income
- Demand for tourist services
- Greater late-game economic potential

Large Investor cities naturally pair well with cultural districts.

---

# Research Synergy

Some collections provide bonuses related to Scholars and the Research Institute.

The guide specifically notes that certain Enbesa-related museum collections increase research generated by Scholar residences. :contentReference[oaicite:2]{index=2}

---

# Layout Planning

Rather than scattering cultural buildings throughout the city, many players create a dedicated cultural district.

Example:

```text
Museum

↓

Zoo

↓

Botanical Garden

↓

Monuments

↓

Parks
```

Centralized placement simplifies future expansion.

---

# Item Acquisition

Collection items are commonly obtained through:

- Expeditions
- Quests
- NPC traders
- Research Institute (late game)

The guide presents these systems as the primary methods for completing collections. :contentReference[oaicite:3]{index=3}

---

# Expansion

As additional items are acquired:

Expand by:

- adding exhibition modules
- completing new collections
- reorganizing layouts

Leave sufficient space for future exhibits.

---

# Cape Trelawney

The enormous central island of Cape Trelawney is particularly well suited for large cultural districts due to its abundant available space.

The guide recommends using Cape Trelawney as a location for major cultural developments and monuments. :contentReference[oaicite:4]{index=4}

---

# Optimization

Late-game cultural districts often combine:

- Museum
- Zoo
- Botanical Garden
- World's Fair
- Parks
- Tourist infrastructure

Together they create one of the most attractive areas in the empire.

---

# Common Mistakes

⚠️ Ignoring collections.

⚠️ Displaying random items instead of completing sets.

⚠️ Leaving no room for expansion.

⚠️ Building cultural districts beside heavy industry.

⚠️ Overlooking research bonuses from specific collections.

⚠️ Treating cultural buildings as purely decorative.

---

---

---

# Key Takeaways

- Cultural buildings become increasingly valuable as the game progresses.
- Completing collections is far more important than displaying isolated items.
- Museums, Zoos, and Botanical Gardens significantly increase attractiveness and unlock powerful empire-wide bonuses.
- Some collections directly improve Scholar research, linking culture with late-game optimization.
- A dedicated cultural district is easier to expand and provides a strong foundation for tourism and prestige.
