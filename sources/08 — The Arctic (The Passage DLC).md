# 08 — The Arctic (The Passage DLC)

> The **Arctic** is introduced in **The Passage** DLC and presents one of the most unique logistical challenges in Anno 1800. Unlike other regions, survival depends not only on supplying goods but also on maintaining **heat**. Its settlements are small, specialized, and primarily focused on extracting valuable late-game resources.

This chapter covers the Arctic's population, mechanics, production, logistics, and strategic importance.

---

# Overview

The Arctic is unlocked through **The Passage** DLC.

Once Engineers are established in the Old World, an expedition becomes available, granting access to the new polar region. :contentReference[oaicite:0]{index=0}

Unlike other sessions:

- only two population tiers exist
- heat replaces happiness
- construction materials are simplified
- settlements are much smaller
- logistics become significantly more demanding

---

# Population

The Arctic contains only two population classes.

```text
Explorers

↓

Technicians
```

Explorers form the foundation of every Arctic settlement.

Technicians unlock advanced production, infrastructure, and airship technology. :contentReference[oaicite:1]{index=1}

---

# Heat

Heat is the defining mechanic of the Arctic.

Instead of happiness, almost every building requires heat to function.

Without sufficient heating:

- production stops
- disease spreads
- population declines
- buildings become ineffective

The guide emphasizes that residences and production buildings require heating supplied by stoves. :contentReference[oaicite:2]{index=2}

---

# Heating Network

Basic flow:

```text
Coal

↓

Stove

↓

Heat Radius

↓

Residences

Factories

Public Buildings
```

Every expansion should begin by ensuring that new buildings remain inside a heated area.

---

# Construction Materials

Unlike the Old World, Arctic construction is simplified.

Primary materials include:

- Timber
- Steel Beams (imported)

The guide notes that steel beams must be imported from the Old World. :contentReference[oaicite:3]{index=3}

---

# Early Settlement

Recommended priorities:

1. Trading Post
2. Explorer Shelters
3. Stove
4. Hunter buildings
5. Basic food
6. Fire and medical coverage

Expansion without adequate heating should be avoided.

---

# Explorer Needs

Explorers require goods that provide both survival and warmth.

Examples described in the guide include:

- Pemmican
- Sleeping Bags
- Oil Lamps
- Liquor (later)

These needs are introduced gradually as the population grows. :contentReference[oaicite:4]{index=4}

# Technicians

Upgrading to Technicians unlocks advanced Arctic infrastructure.

Key unlocks include:

- Airship Hangar
- Arctic Lodge
- Air Mail
- Advanced hunting
- Polar Bear production
- Sled production
- Gas extraction

The guide identifies Technicians as the tier that unlocks these advanced buildings and industries. :contentReference[oaicite:8]{index=8}

---

# Airship Hangar

One of the Arctic's most important buildings.

Functions:

- constructs airships
- serves as the center of aerial logistics
- requires multiple construction phases
- consumes advanced materials
- requires heat

Only one hangar may be built per island. :contentReference[oaicite:9]{index=9}

---

# Airships

Airships differ from ships in several ways.

Advantages:

- ignore naval routes
- faster transportation
- access remote Arctic islands
- useful for difficult terrain

Disadvantages:

- lower cargo capacity
- expensive construction
- advanced materials required

The guide later describes airship items that improve speed, loading, maintenance, and expedition performance. :contentReference[oaicite:10]{index=10}

---

# Arctic Lodge

- Trade Union
- Town Hall

# Logistics

The Arctic is heavily dependent on imports.

Typical imports:

- Steel Beams
- Brass
- Canned Food
- Coffee
- Liquor

As settlements develop, additional Old World products become mandatory for higher population tiers. :contentReference[oaicite:14]{index=14}

# Common Mistakes

⚠️ Expanding outside heated areas.

⚠️ Forgetting imported construction materials.

⚠️ Running out of coal.

⚠️ Building oversized settlements.

⚠️ Treating the Arctic like a normal Old World region.

⚠️ Delaying gas production.

# Key Takeaways

- The Arctic is a survival-focused logistics region rather than a population center.
- Heat is the central mechanic and must be planned before expansion.
- Most advanced production depends on imports from the Old World.
- Technicians unlock the Arctic's most valuable technologies, including airships and gas extraction.
- The region's greatest strategic value lies in supplying unique late-game resources rather than supporting large cities.
