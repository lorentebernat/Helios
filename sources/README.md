# Gameplay Overview

At its core, Anno 1800 is an economy and logistics simulation.

The gameplay loop is built around:

1. Colonize islands.
2. Produce construction materials.
3. Expand your population.
4. Unlock higher population tiers.
5. Produce increasingly complex goods.
6. Establish trade routes between regions.
7. Optimize production.
8. Increase income and influence.
9. Expand into new regions.
10. Develop a highly efficient global empire.

As your cities grow, each new population tier introduces additional needs, buildings, production chains, and mechanics. The guide presents progression through the Old World before expanding into other regions and later-game systems. :contentReference[oaicite:1]{index=1}

# Core Principles

The guide consistently reinforces several strategic ideas:

- Expand steadily rather than rapidly.
- Meet population needs before upgrading residences.
- Build production only when demand requires it.
- Use multiple islands to compensate for missing fertilities and resources.
- Connect regions with reliable trade routes as new goods become necessary.
- Improve efficiency through items, electricity, and later-game systems as they unlock. :contentReference[oaicite:7]{index=7}
