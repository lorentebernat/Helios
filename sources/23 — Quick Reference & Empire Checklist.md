```text
Farmers
    ↓
Workers
    ↓
Artisans
    ↓
Engineers
    ↓
Investors
```

```text

Trade Union
```

These systems provide the largest production improvements.

# Logistics Priorities

Every trade route should:

- Carry useful cargo
- Avoid unnecessary stops
- Use appropriate ships
- Maintain stable inventories

Never allow logistics to become the limiting factor.

---

# Harbor Priorities

Essential harbor infrastructure:

- Trading Post
- Depots
- Additional Piers
- Repair Crane
- Harbormaster's Office

Busy harbors should always have room for expansion.

# Research Checklist

Before investing Research Points:

- Prioritize frequently used specialists.
- Duplicate powerful Trade Union items.
- Improve logistics before researching niche projects.

Research should reinforce the empire's largest production chains.

# Trade Route Audit

Ask regularly:

- Are ships full?
- Are routes overloaded?
- Are warehouses congested?
- Are imports balanced?
- Are exports stable?

Many shortages originate in transport rather than production.

---

# Common Bottlenecks

Most frequent causes of shortages:

1. Warehouse congestion
2. Missing workforce
3. Harbor congestion
4. Insufficient shipping
5. Missing electricity
6. Broken production chains

The guide repeatedly emphasizes diagnosing bottlenecks before expanding production. :contentReference[oaicite:1]{index=1}

---

# Expansion Checklist

Before settling another island:

- [ ] Positive income
- [ ] Stable workforce
- [ ] Construction materials available
- [ ] Shipping capacity available

Expansion should solve an economic problem—not create one.

---

# Late-Game Priorities

Order of importance:

1. Stable logistics
2. Electricity
3. Specialists
4. Research
5. Cultural bonuses
6. Tourism
7. Further expansion

Optimization consistently provides better returns than uncontrolled growth.

# Final Summary

The guide presents **Anno 1800** as a game of continuous optimization rather than continuous expansion.

Successful empires consistently follow the same cycle:

```text
Stable Economy

↓

Expansion

↓

Specialization

↓

Trade

↓

Optimization

↓

Research

↓

Higher Efficiency

↓

Repeat
```

Every major mechanic—population, production, logistics, electricity, specialists, culture, and research—contributes to a single objective:

> **Build a highly efficient, interconnected empire where every island performs a specialized role and every production chain supports the whole.**
