# 24 — Production Chain Encyclopedia

> This chapter serves as a reference for the production chains described throughout the source guide. Rather than teaching progression, it summarizes how major goods are produced, what they require, and the strategic role they play within your empire.

> **Note:** This chapter reflects the production chains and progression presented in the source guide. It is not intended to be an exhaustive wiki of every recipe or DLC variation.

---

# How to Read a Production Chain

Every production chain follows the same structure:

```text
Raw Materials

↓

Intermediate Goods

↓

Finished Product

↓

Population Need
```

As populations advance, production chains generally become:

- Longer
- More specialized
- More dependent on imports
- More valuable

# Optimization Priorities

The guide consistently recommends improving production using:

1. Electricity
2. Trade Unions
3. Specialists
4. Warehouse placement
5. Efficient logistics

Optimization is generally more valuable than simply building additional factories.

# Production Chain Principles

Most advanced goods follow this pattern:

```text
Agriculture

↓

Processing

↓

Manufacturing

↓

Luxury Product

↓

Population
```

As the game progresses:

- Production chains become longer.
- More imported materials are required.
- Logistics become increasingly important.
- Specialists provide larger efficiency gains.

---

# Common Bottlenecks

Typical production problems include:

⚠️ Missing workforce.

⚠️ Warehouse congestion.

⚠️ Missing imported goods.

⚠️ Insufficient shipping.

⚠️ No electricity.

⚠️ Poor Trade Union coverage.

Most shortages originate from logistics rather than insufficient production.

---

# Production Audit

For every production chain, verify:

- [ ] Raw materials available.
- [ ] Intermediate products stable.
- [ ] Workforce sufficient.
- [ ] Warehouse nearby.
- [ ] Electricity available (where applicable).
- [ ] Trade Union coverage.
- [ ] Shipping capacity sufficient.
- [ ] Consumer demand satisfied.

---

# Key Takeaways

- Every production chain ultimately converts raw resources into population needs.
- Construction materials enable expansion, while luxury goods drive late-game prosperity.
- New World imports become essential for advanced production.
- Electricity, specialists, and Trade Unions dramatically increase production efficiency.
- The strongest production chains are those supported by reliable logistics and careful planning.
