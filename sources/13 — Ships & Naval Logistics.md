# 13 — Ships & Naval Logistics

> Ships are the arteries of your empire. They transport goods, colonize new islands, support expeditions, defend trade routes, and project military power across every region. As your empire grows, fleet management becomes just as important as city management.

This chapter summarizes the ship classes and naval logistics described throughout the source guide.

---

# Overview

Ships fulfill five major roles:

- Exploration
- Trade
- Colonization
- Military
- Expeditions

Early fleets are relatively small, but late-game empires often operate dozens of specialized vessels simultaneously.

---

# Fleet Evolution

Typical progression:

```text
Small Sailing Fleet

↓

Cargo Fleet

↓

Steam Fleet

↓

Specialized Fleets

↓

Global Logistics Network
```

Steam technology eventually replaces most sailing vessels for long-distance logistics. The guide introduces steam-powered ships as advanced upgrades with greater efficiency and capability. :contentReference[oaicite:0]{index=0}

# Sailing Ships

Advantages:

- inexpensive
- available early
- no fuel requirements

Disadvantages:

- slower
- lower cargo capacity
- wind dependent

Sailing ships dominate the early game before steam technology becomes available.

---

# Steam Ships

Steam ships represent a major technological leap.

Advantages:

- higher speed
- consistent movement
- greater cargo capacity
- stronger combat performance

Disadvantages:

- higher construction costs
- advanced materials required

The guide presents steam vessels as the preferred option for mature logistics networks. :contentReference[oaicite:1]{index=1}

---

# Cargo Ships

Primary purpose:

```text
Warehouse

↓

Harbor

↓

Cargo Ship

↓

Destination Harbor

↓

Warehouse
```

Cargo ships should spend as much time moving goods as possible.

Idle ships reduce overall logistical efficiency.

---

# Cargo Capacity

Higher cargo capacity generally means:

- fewer trips
- lower transportation costs
- more stable inventories

Long-distance trade routes particularly benefit from larger vessels.

---

# Oil Tankers

Oil transport eventually becomes essential.

Typical route:

```text
Oil Harbor

↓

Oil Tanker

↓

Power Plant Harbor

↓

Railway

↓

Power Plant
```

Without reliable oil deliveries, electricity production stops.

---

# Airships

Unlocked through **The Passage** DLC.

Advantages:

- ignore naval routes
- rapid transport
- Arctic access
- flexible logistics

Limitations:

- lower cargo capacity
- expensive infrastructure
- advanced production requirements

Airships complement traditional shipping rather than replacing it. :contentReference[oaicite:2]{index=2}

---

# Colonization Ships

New islands require ships capable of transporting:

- construction materials
- settlers
- supplies

Typical early cargo:

- Timber
- Steel Beams
- Food

Rapid colony establishment depends on reliable transport.

# Expedition Ships

Expeditions unlock:

- new regions
- specialist items

- storyline progression

The guide includes expedition mechanics as part of regional expansion into DLC sessions. :contentReference[oaicite:3]{index=3}

---

# Harbor Operations

Every cargo ship follows the same cycle.

```text
Arrive

↓

Unload

↓

Load

↓

Depart
```

Congested harbors dramatically reduce fleet efficiency.

Additional piers and optimized scheduling help reduce delays.

---

Ships can equip specialist items.

Possible bonuses include:

# Fleet Maintenance

As fleets expand:

Monitor:

- idle ships
- duplicated routes
- overloaded routes
- underused cargo capacity

Removing unnecessary vessels can significantly reduce maintenance costs.

---

# Convoys

Busy trade routes benefit from:

- escort ships
- patrol fleets
- protected shipping lanes

Military protection becomes increasingly important when hostile AI or pirates threaten commerce.

---

# Late-Game Fleet

A mature empire commonly operates:

- Local cargo ships
- Steam cargo ships
- Oil tankers
- Military fleets
- Expedition vessels
- Airships

Each fleet serves a dedicated logistical or military purpose.

---

# Common Mistakes

⚠️ Using small ships on major trade routes.

⚠️ Allowing ships to sail half empty.

⚠️ Creating unnecessarily complex routes.

⚠️ Ignoring harbor congestion.

⚠️ Using one fleet for every logistical task.

⚠️ Neglecting replacement of sailing ships with steam vessels as the economy grows.

# Key Takeaways

- Ships connect every island and region into a single functioning economy.
- Steam vessels become the backbone of mature logistics networks.
- Specialized fleets are more efficient than multipurpose fleets.
- Harbor throughput is often the limiting factor in naval logistics.
- Efficient fleet management improves both economic stability and military security.
