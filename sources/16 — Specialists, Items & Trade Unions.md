# 16 — Specialists, Items & Trade Unions

> Specialists and items are among the most powerful optimization systems in **Anno 1800**. While a functional economy can succeed without them, an optimized empire relies heavily on carefully selected specialists to improve production, reduce consumption, unlock alternate recipes, and dramatically increase efficiency.

This chapter summarizes the item system and specialist buildings described throughout the source guide.

---

# Overview

Items provide bonuses to:

- Production
- Residences
- Harbors
- Ships
- Expeditions
- Military
- Agriculture
- Culture

Most powerful items are activated by placing them inside dedicated buildings.

The guide contains extensive catalogs of specialists and item effects across production, ships, expeditions, and cultural buildings. :contentReference[oaicite:0]{index=0}

---

# Item Buildings

Four major buildings use specialists.

```text
Trade Union

↓

Production
```

```text
Town Hall

↓

Residences
```

```text
Harbormaster's Office

↓

Harbor Buildings
```

```text
Arctic Lodge

↓

Arctic Buildings
```

Each affects buildings within its influence radius.

---

# Trade Union

Trade Unions improve nearby production buildings.

Possible bonuses include:

- Increased productivity
- Reduced workforce
- Reduced maintenance
- Additional goods
- Alternative production recipes
- Increased storage

Factories should be arranged around Trade Unions to maximize coverage.

The guide lists numerous Trade Union specialists that modify industrial production. :contentReference[oaicite:1]{index=1}

---

# Productivity Bonuses

A specialist may increase production by:

```text
100%

↓

125%

↓

150%

↓

200%+
```

Higher productivity means:

- more output
- fewer buildings required
- lower maintenance per unit produced

---

# Additional Goods

Some specialists generate bonus products.

Example concept:

```text
Steel Factory

↓

Steel

+

Occasional Extra Product
```

Additional goods are effectively "free" production and are among the strongest item effects.

---

# Alternative Recipes

Certain specialists replace expensive inputs.

Example concept:

```text
Original Recipe

Input A

+

Input B

↓

Output
```

becomes

```text
Alternative Recipe

Input C

↓

Output
```

Alternative recipes can simplify logistics and reduce dependence on scarce resources.

---

# Workforce Reduction

Some specialists reduce workforce requirements.

Benefits:

- easier expansion
- lower workforce pressure
- more flexible population planning

---

# Maintenance Reduction

Maintenance bonuses reduce operating costs.

Benefits include:

- higher profits
- easier early expansion
- more efficient industry

---

# Town Hall

Town Halls affect nearby residences.

Typical bonuses include:

- Increased population
- Reduced consumption
- Increased happiness
- Higher tax income
- Improved attractiveness

Dense residential districts benefit the most.

The guide catalogs numerous Town Hall specialists for residential optimization. :contentReference[oaicite:2]{index=2}

---

# Harbormaster's Office

Harbormaster's Offices improve harbor operations.

Possible effects:

- Faster loading
- Reduced maintenance
- Additional goods
- Better repairs
- Improved harbor defenses

Large trade hubs benefit greatly from optimized harbor specialists.

---

# Arctic Lodge

Exclusive to **The Passage** DLC.

Functions similarly to:

- Trade Union
- Town Hall

It enhances nearby Arctic buildings using specialist items. :contentReference[oaicite:3]{index=3}

---

# Ship Items

Ships may equip specialized items.

Possible bonuses:

- Increased speed
- Faster loading
- Improved combat
- Better expedition performance
- Reduced maintenance

The guide also lists numerous ship equipment items and captains. :contentReference[oaicite:4]{index=4}

---

# Expedition Items

Certain specialists excel during expeditions.

Examples of bonuses:

- Navigation
- Diplomacy
- Hunting
- Medicine
- Faith
- Crafting

Selecting complementary expedition items greatly increases mission success rates.

---

# Cultural Items

Museums, Zoos, and Botanical Gardens accept collection items.

Completing collections grants:

- Attractiveness
- Population bonuses
- Productivity bonuses
- Research bonuses
- Tourism benefits

The guide identifies collection completion as an important source of empire-wide bonuses. :contentReference[oaicite:5]{index=5}

---

# Research Institute

With the **Land of Lions** DLC, the Research Institute allows players to:

- Research items
- Duplicate specialists
- Acquire previously unavailable bonuses

This removes much of the randomness traditionally associated with obtaining rare specialists. :contentReference[oaicite:6]{index=6}

---

# Item Synergy

The strongest layouts combine multiple bonuses.

Example:

```text
Trade Union

↓

+ Productivity

↓

+ Additional Goods

↓

+ Alternative Recipe

↓

Highly Optimized Factory Cluster
```

Layering compatible effects often produces much greater benefits than relying on a single powerful item.

---

# Industrial Planning

When using specialists:

Plan:

- factory clusters
- Trade Union radius
- electricity coverage
- warehouse placement
- railway access

Moving factories later can be costly.

---

# Residential Planning

Dense neighborhoods maximize Town Hall efficiency.

Typical layout:

```text
Town Hall

↓

Engineer Residences

↓

Investor Residences
```

One Town Hall can improve dozens of residences simultaneously.

---

# Harbor Planning

Important harbor buildings should remain inside Harbormaster coverage.

Examples:

- Piers
- Repair Crane
- Oil Harbor
- Depots

Strategic placement increases both logistical efficiency and defensive strength.

---

# Common Mistakes

⚠️ Ignoring specialists entirely.

⚠️ Placing factories outside Trade Union range.

⚠️ Mixing unrelated factories under the same Trade Union.

⚠️ Using Town Halls in sparse residential areas.

⚠️ Forgetting to optimize harbor buildings.

⚠️ Neglecting Research Institute item duplication.

# Key Takeaways

- Specialists are one of the largest sources of efficiency in Anno 1800.
- Trade Unions, Town Halls, Harbormaster's Offices, and Arctic Lodges each optimize different parts of your empire.
- Additional goods and alternative recipes often provide the greatest long-term value.
- The Research Institute transforms rare specialists into reproducible strategic assets.
- Careful planning around specialist coverage can dramatically reduce costs while increasing production across the entire empire.
