# 28 — Collection Encyclopedia

> This chapter summarizes the cultural collection system presented throughout the source guide. Collections are one of the principal long-term progression systems in **Anno 1800**, providing powerful passive bonuses while increasing the attractiveness and prestige of your cities.

> **Note:** The source guide discusses the collection system and its strategic value, but it does **not** provide a complete list of every individual collection piece.

---

# Overview

Collections are displayed in three cultural buildings:

- Museum
- Zoo
- Botanical Garden

Each completed collection grants permanent bonuses in addition to increasing city attractiveness.

The guide identifies collections as one of the primary long-term goals alongside expeditions and research. :contentReference[oaicite:0]{index=0}

---

# Sources of Collection Items

Collection pieces can be obtained from:

- Expeditions
- Quests
- World events
- Traders
- Research Institute
- Rewards

Many rare pieces are unique or significantly easier to acquire through expeditions.

---

# Museum

## Theme

Historical artifacts

Examples include:

- Archaeology
- Ancient civilizations
- Scientific discoveries
- Historical relics

---

## Benefits

Museums provide:

- Attractiveness
- Collection bonuses
- Tourism support

Some collections also improve production or research.

The guide references museum collections that enhance Scholar research generation. :contentReference[oaicite:1]{index=1}

---

# Zoo

## Theme

Animals from around the world.

Animals are primarily obtained through:

- Expeditions
- Quests
- Special rewards

---

## Benefits

Zoo collections improve:

- Attractiveness
- Tourism
- Prestige

They also contribute toward city attractiveness milestones.

---

# Botanical Garden

## Theme

Plants and botanical specimens.

Introduced with the **Botanica** DLC.

---

## Benefits

Provides:

- Attractiveness
- Collection bonuses
- Tourism

Completes the trio of cultural districts.

---

# Collection Bonuses

Collections often reward:

- Increased attractiveness
- Production bonuses
- Population bonuses
- Research bonuses
- Tourism improvements

The most valuable bonuses are typically unlocked only after completing an entire themed set.

---

# Attractiveness

Collections are one of the largest contributors to city attractiveness.

Higher attractiveness improves:

- Tourism
- Prestige
- Overall city value

Large cultural districts are therefore most effective when concentrated in major population centers.

---

# Tourism

Tourists are attracted by:

- Museums
- Zoos
- Botanical Gardens
- Monuments
- Beautiful cities

The guide presents tourism as a late-game extension of attractiveness rather than an isolated mechanic.

---

# Research Synergy

Certain museum collections provide bonuses that directly affect Scholars and research output.

Typical progression:

```text
Museum Collection

↓

Research Bonus

↓

More Research Points

↓

Faster Optimization
```

This creates an important connection between cultural development and technological progress.

---

# Expedition Synergy

Expeditions are one of the primary methods of acquiring:

- Museum artifacts
- Zoo animals
- Botanical specimens

Typical loop:

```text
Expedition

↓

Collection Item

↓

Complete Collection

↓

Permanent Bonus
```

This makes exploration a critical part of cultural progression.

---

# Research Institute Synergy

The Research Institute can reduce dependence on random collection acquisition by allowing certain cultural items to be researched or reproduced.

This supports faster completion of valuable collections during the late game. :contentReference[oaicite:2]{index=2}

---

# Cultural District Planning

An efficient cultural district usually combines:

```text
Museum

+

Zoo

+

Botanical Garden

+

Parks

+

Monuments

↓

High Attractiveness
```

These districts are best located near Investor and Tourist areas.

---

# Priorities

Recommended order:

1. Complete high-value collections.
2. Focus on permanent bonuses rather than random displays.
3. Improve attractiveness.
4. Support tourism.
5. Enhance research where possible.

Completing a collection is almost always more valuable than displaying unrelated pieces.

---

# Common Mistakes

⚠️ Displaying incomplete collections without pursuing bonuses.

⚠️ Selling rare collection items.

⚠️ Ignoring expeditions that reward cultural items.

⚠️ Scattering cultural buildings across multiple islands.

⚠️ Neglecting attractiveness in late-game cities.

---

# Key Takeaways

- Museums, Zoos, and Botanical Gardens are long-term investments that reward complete collections with permanent bonuses.
- Expeditions are the primary source of many rare collection items.
- Cultural development increases attractiveness, supports tourism, and can even enhance research.
- The Research Institute reduces the randomness involved in completing valuable collections.
- Well-planned cultural districts become an important component of a fully optimized late-game empire.
